# Nook privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Nook does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Everything you add to Nook is stored only on your device. Nothing you add is
sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Nook may connect to the internet to check for app updates. That check contains
no personal information and nothing you have added to Nook.

The camera, microphone and photo library are used only to add photos and videos
to Nook. Notifications are used only for the reminder you can turn on in
Settings.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here.
